## Overview

KunPeng is built for anyone studying or reading Chinese who wants lookups to feel immediate, distraction-free, and dependable anywhere—no accounts, no internet required, and zero clutter.

Search in **Chinese, pinyin, or English**; inspect the components of an unfamiliar character; and save what you learn to custom lists or flashcard decks.

## Features

- **Search without a connection**: Look up over 120,000 Chinese characters, pinyin, and English meanings with the built-in CC-CEDICT dictionary.
- **Write to search**: Draw characters directly on screen with responsive on-device handwriting recognition to find words when you don't know the pinyin.
- **See stroke order**: Watch stroke animations for supported characters and practice drawing them stroke by stroke.
- **Memory help & radicals**: Break characters into components, explore Kangxi radicals, and understand character structure.
- **Review with flashcards**: Study saved words, swipe through decks, retry difficult cards, and pick up right where you left off.
- **Build word lists**: Bookmark entries, use built-in HSK lists, create your own collections, and import or export lists.
- **Clear audio & tone cues**: Listen to pronunciations with adjustable playback speed, and choose colored pinyin or compact tone bars.
- **Clean, comfortable design**: Light, dark, and system themes tailored for relaxed reading and zero eye strain.

## Download and install

1. Open the [latest GitHub release](https://github.com/EmpyrealWhale/kunpeng/releases/latest).
2. Download the APK for your device (or the universal APK).
3. Open the downloaded APK on your Android device and install it (allow installing unknown apps if prompted).

> Requires **Android 8.0** or newer. Dictionary searches and installed handwriting models work completely offline.
