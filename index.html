FAQ

Common questions

For suggestions, corrections, or bug reports, use Send feedback below.

01Is KunPeng free?

Yes. The app is free to download and use.

02Can I use it offline?

Dictionary searches and saved vocabulary work offline. Handwriting recognition works offline after the recognition model has been downloaded once.

03How do I review saved vocabulary?

Bookmark entries or add words to lists, then use flashcards to review them.

04Does it support traditional Chinese?

Yes. You can show traditional characters in the app settings.

05How do I install it?

Download the APK from the latest GitHub release and open it on your Android device.
